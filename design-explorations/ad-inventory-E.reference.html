What works

It's automatically relevant: trail shoes on a mountain race, road racers on a marathon. Race type and distances already exist on every race, so targeting comes free, and it scales to any number of shops.

What risks

It's the heaviest to build (feed ingestion, targeting rules, tracking). Revenue depends on clicks, and the tiles are visually busy on mobile.

Pick this if

you want ongoing, scalable revenue from running shops and are willing to build a feed integration.
